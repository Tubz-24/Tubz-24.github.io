Physics-Based Combat Demo

Physics is underused in hand-to-hand combat — most games fake the impact. This demo tests the alternative: a sword encounter where swing force is calculated from classical-mechanics formulas, so how you move the weapon is what determines how hard it hits.

I researched physics implementations across Super Mario Bros., Pong, Portal and Star Wars: The Force Unleashed, then built the movement and swing-force system over four weeks. A five-person playtest group confirmed the core concept worked.
