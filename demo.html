Physics-Based Combat Demo

Swing force is calculated from classical-mechanics formulas, so how you move the weapon decides how hard it hits. Try a slow swing, then a fast one.

Best on desktop with a mouse and keyboard. First load can take a few seconds while the engine starts up.

What you're looking at

Impact you can feel, not just see.

Instead of playing a canned hit animation, the demo reads the motion of your swing and calculates the force with real mechanics. Faster, heavier swings land harder. Two enemies, one sword, and a physics system doing the work.
