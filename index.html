Physics-Based Combat Demo

Final year project · Unity

Physics is underused in hand-to-hand combat systems — most games fake the impact. This demo tests the alternative: a sword-combat encounter where swing force is calculated from real classical-mechanics formulas, so how you move the weapon is what determines how hard it hits.
